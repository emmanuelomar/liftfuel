Lift & Fuel · Security

What we do with your data

Your log lives
on your phone.

No account to make. No ads. No tracking. Lift & Fuel works fully offline, and your training and food records stay on your device unless you decide to send them somewhere yourself.

No account
There is no sign up and no login. You open the app and start.
No tracking
No ads, no analytics, no third party watching what you do.
Works offline
Everything runs on the phone. The network is only used for the two things below.

01 · Where your data goes

Every place the app can reach, and what it sends.

Two of these five stay entirely on the phone. The other two only ever happen because you asked for them, and they send the least possible.

Your training and food log

Every workout, set, meal, macro, weigh in, reminder and routine is saved in a database on your phone, protected by your iPhone's own security. By default it is never uploaded anywhere.

On this phone

Apple Health

If you connect Health, the app reads your weight to work out your daily burn, and writes back the weigh ins and workouts you log so your other apps can see them. This reading and writing happens on the device.

On this phone
Health data never leaves the phone, even when Server Sync is on.

Camera

The camera turns on only while the barcode scanner is open, and only to read a product barcode. Nothing is photographed, recorded or kept.

On this phone

Barcode lookups

When you scan a barcode, the app looks the product up in Open Food Facts, a public food database. Only the barcode number is sent. Nothing about you goes with it, and there is no account.

Leaves the phone
Sends: a barcode number → To: Open Food Facts

Server Sync · OFF BY DEFAULT

Server Sync is switched off until you turn it on, and you point it at a server you run yourself. When it is on, the app sends a backup of your records over an encrypted connection and downloads approved reference updates. The backup is a copy only, so the server can never change what is on your phone. Your sync token, and any weigh ins imported from Health, are deliberately left out of every backup.

Leaves the phone
Only if you enable it → To: a server you choose

02 · What Lift & Fuel never does

The lines the app does not cross.

  • ✕No advertising. There are no ads and no ad networks in the app.
  • ✕No analytics. Your taps and screens are not measured or reported.
  • ✕No behavioural tracking. Nothing profiles you across apps or sites.
  • ✕No selling or sharing. Your data is not a product and is never sold.
  • ✕No third party logins. There is no account to link and none is asked for.

03 · You are in control

It is your data, and it stays that way.

Take it with you

Export a full copy of everything the app holds whenever you want it.

Remove it completely

Your records live on the phone. Deleting them, or removing the app, takes the data with it.

Sync is your choice

Server Sync started off. You can turn it on, and turn it back off, at any time.

Health stays put

You grant Health access, and you can withdraw it in the Health app. Those readings never leave the phone.
