3. How information is collected

App information is collected directly from what you type, select or log.

Apple Health. If you grant permission, Lift & Fuel reads from Apple Health: your body weight, to help work out your daily energy use; how long you slept last night, which is shown on the Recover screen and used in its readiness estimate; and, only if you choose Apple Health as your period source in the Cycle card, your logged menstrual flow, so the app can place today in your cycle. The period read is a separate, additional permission that iOS asks for at the moment you choose it; declining it leaves every other feature working. The phone's existing logging features write back the weigh-ins you enter and the strength workouts you save, so your other apps can see them; it never writes sleep or cycle information to Apple Health. Weight, sleep and cycle information read from Apple Health stays on your device: it is never included in the automatic backup file, never included in your export, and never sent anywhere by the app. It is kept in a separate database, in a folder that is not open to the Files app and is excluded from your device's iCloud and computer backups.

Daily steps from Apple Health. In Settings you can separately opt in to show Apple Health step totals on Today, including steps from your paired Apple Watch once Apple has synced them to your iPhone. This asks for read-only Steps permission. The app uses Health's combined total instead of adding phone and Watch counts together. It refreshes while Today is open and when the app returns to the foreground; syncing is not guaranteed to be immediate. These totals are held only in screen memory, cleared when the app goes into the background, and are not sent to Friends, widgets, backups or exports. They do not change calorie targets. Turn this option off in Settings to stop these reads; you can also revoke Steps permission in Apple Health. The separate Motion & Fitness option below continues to use only the phone sensor for optional Friends sharing.

Running and walking imports. You can request a manual import of completed running workouts and your Lift & Fuel Watch walks, with a separate choice to include their routes. Alternatively, you can explicitly enable “Automatically sync runs & routes” in Run & walk and grant Apple Health permissions. Automatic sync is off by default. When enabled, it checks the last 90 days of eligible completed workouts and their available routes, including routes that arrive after the workout. It may refresh when you open the app and when Apple delivers a background Health update; immediate background delivery is not guaranteed. Runs started in Apple's Workout app or another app are imported after completion, not accessed as a live session. Turning automatic sync off stops these automatic reads and live Watch previews, without deleting previously imported records or stopping a Watch workout. Imported workouts are kept in the app's private, backup-excluded native storage. Completed runs from other apps also inform recovery, Coach recommendations and training-day calorie targets privately on this phone. That working summary is held only in memory; it is not copied to the ordinary Cardio log, backups, exports, widgets, Friends or Stories. Your own Lift & Fuel Watch runs and walks may appear in Cardio and may be shared as an image only when you choose Share. No route or heart-rate data is sent to Friends. Declining Health access leaves manual logging and phone running available.

Location and run recording. Starting an outdoor run or walk asks for location permission. While that session is recording, location points, their times and accuracy are used to calculate distance, pace, splits and your route. Recording may continue with the screen locked or another app open; it is not continuous location tracking outside a session. You can pause or finish the session. Indoor sessions do not need a GPS route and allow typed distance. Routes and recovery checkpoints are saved in protected app-private native files excluded from normal device backups and app exports. Only your own completed-session summary, without route coordinates or heart rate, joins the ordinary Cardio log and its backups.

Maps and Stories. The live map while running, opening a saved route map or making a map Story uses Apple's map service; map requests disclose the area being displayed to Apple under Apple's privacy practices. The private map can show the full recorded route; start and finish concealment applies to the shared Story, not to your private route view. A route-only or splits Story does not require a map background. Sharing is always a separate action using the system share sheet. Start and finish concealment removes the selected ends of the displayed route, but cannot guarantee anonymity: route shape, landmarks, text or a photo may identify a place. Review the actual preview before sharing. The photo picker reads only the photo you choose; the app re-renders the image without copying its original location metadata. Copies saved or shared outside the app cannot be recalled by deleting the run.

Apple Watch. The companion receives the current strength session's title, exercise names, load, repetitions and rest information from the paired iPhone. Log, correction, undo and timer commands are queued privately until the phone confirms the save. If you choose Watch recording and grant Health permission, HealthKit reads heart rate and active energy; running and walking also use walking/running distance. It writes the workout and active energy, with a workout route for an outdoor recording. Heart rate and route coordinates are not sent through the strength-control connection or to Friends. Separately, when automatic run sync is enabled on the phone, a live Lift & Fuel Watch run or walk can mirror its session state, distance and timing totals, and bounded batches of outdoor route coordinates, timestamps and accuracy to the paired iPhone through Apple's workout-mirroring connection. This live preview does not start a second phone recording, and it does not send a heart-rate series to the phone interface. You may pause or resume the connected Watch run from the phone; finish it on Watch. Missing or delayed updates are not filled with an invented route. Completed records are reconciled through the Health import described above. Live route data does not enter Friends, widgets or ordinary app backups. Each device keeps its snapshot and queued commands in its own backup-excluded application storage. Clearing phone data invalidates that command history; an offline Watch retains its old local copy until it reconnects and receives the reset. Workouts already saved in Apple Health remain there and can be managed through Health. Watch complications may display workout information on the watch face.

Motion & Fitness. If you turn the step counter on, the app asks iOS for Motion & Fitness permission and reads your step count, and the steps and distance recorded while a cardio timer is running, from the phone's own motion sensor. This is shown to you and used to estimate the distance of a walk or run. A daily step total is shared only if you separately consent to a Friends steps challenge (section 8). It never changes your calorie target. The feature is off until you turn it on, and declining the permission leaves cardio exactly as it was: minutes, a typed distance and an estimate from time.

Face ID and your device passcode. If you turn on the optional lock for cycle data, the app asks iOS to confirm you are the device owner using Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode. iOS performs that check; the app receives only a pass or fail result and never sees your face data, fingerprint or passcode.

Camera. The camera is used only while the barcode scanner is open, to read a product barcode. Nothing is photographed, recorded or stored. What happens to the number it reads, and the limits of what comes back, are set out in section 7.

Lift & Fuel does not access your contacts. Precise location is optional and used for the run recording described above. It does not use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs or cross-app tracking. Notification permission is requested when you first start a workout, so the rest timer can alert you, when you choose to schedule reminders, or when you separately choose Circle chat alerts for this phone (section 8).

5. Storage on your phone

Your personal profile and logs are stored in the app's database on your device. Only the separately consented Friends information in section 8 is sent to our service. Protect your phone with a passcode and keep its operating system current.

The app's files are visible in the Files app. Lift & Fuel keeps its main database and its automatic daily backups in its own folder on your phone, and that folder is open to the Files app under On My iPhone → Lift & Fuel. This is deliberate, so a backup is yours to copy, move or send somewhere safe without going through us. It also means anyone who can unlock your phone can read, copy or delete those files, including the cycle records described in section 6, without opening the app. Information read from Apple Health is not in that folder (section 3). Nothing is encrypted separately from the phone's own encryption. If that matters to you, a device passcode and Face ID or Touch ID are what protect it.

The Home Screen widget and the Live Activity. The rest timer, with the name of the exercise the rest is for, and the cardio timer, with the name of the activity, can appear on your Lock Screen as a Live Activity, and a small widget can show the day's numbers on your Home Screen. iOS runs these as a separate extension, so the few details they display — the rest countdown or cardio time with that exercise or activity name, which can be a name you typed yourself, and, for today only, the calories and protein still left against your target, the number of sets you have logged and your water total — are shared with that extension through a private storage area reserved for this app. It stays on your phone, it is reachable only by Lift & Fuel and its own extension, and it holds only what is on screen: no food log, no history, no cycle information, no profile. Tapping the widget's water button adds that water to today when you next open the app. Removing the widget and turning off Live Activities in iOS Settings stops both.

Widgets may also show selected training, checklist and supplement details, affirmations and consented Friends summaries, including on the Lock Screen where supported. These are kept in the app's private on-device shared storage. No routes, heart-rate series or Cycle details are included in that storage.

6. Cycle tracking

Cycle tracking is off until you turn it on. It is initially offered where the profile sex is female; existing cycle records remain accessible if that profile setting changes. The optional records listed in section 2 are sensitive health information. The ones you log are held in the same on-device database as the rest of your log; period dates read from Apple Health are held in the separate database described in section 3. You can leave any optional field blank.

Records support a personal journal, descriptive summaries, calendar estimates where suitable and a private appointment preview. Missing days are not treated as symptom-free days. Adding symptoms, intensity, daily-life impact or a condition does not change a muscle recovery score, prescribe a workout or diagnose a condition. An existing, separately enabled calorie preference may add a small allowance when a calendar estimate meets its rules. Unsupported life-stage context, reported irregularity or choosing to hide estimates suppresses that estimate and its allowance. Calendar estimates do not establish ovulation or fertility.

Cycle information is treated more restrictively than the rest of your log:

  • cycle records are excluded from the app's automatic daily backup files. They are included in “Export everything” only when you deliberately request and confirm that full export;
  • period dates and flow imported from Apple Health are excluded even from that export and from the appointment preview. Independent flow or symptoms you log yourself are your own records, including when their dates overlap a Health-sourced period;
  • a full export includes your manually entered symptoms, intimate selections, notes and context. It is a readable file, not protected by the in-app cycle lock. Review the export warning and share it only with a recipient you choose;
  • the appointment preview stays inside the cycle section until you choose to copy its selectable text. Notes, intimate information and personal context are omitted by default and have separate inclusion controls. The preview is descriptive, not a clinical assessment;
  • an optional owner check (Face ID, Touch ID or device passcode) hides cycle screens and requires unlocking before preparing a full export that contains cycle history. It is an in-app access gate, not separate encryption of the database, exported files or copies;
  • hiding cycle details does not remove a separately enabled allowance from today's shared calorie totals; otherwise totals could disagree across the app. No cycle symptom penalty is applied to recovery;
  • pausing tracking keeps history. “Delete cycle history” clears dates, daily entries, symptoms, flow and personal context without clearing your other logs, and returns the period source to manual. The app also attempts to remove its own previously created export files that might contain cycle records. Copies you already moved or shared cannot be recalled.

The Learn & care material is bundled with the app and available without entering a diagnosis or unlocking private history. Its references are not fetched in the background. The app does not infer pregnancy or a medical condition from your records, provide contraceptive decisions, or replace clinical assessment.

7. Barcode scanning and Open Food Facts

Barcode lookup is an optional external data request. Exports and copies you deliberately share are separate, as described above. Scanning is optional. You can log every food by hand and never scan anything.

When it happens. Only when you scan a barcode this app does not already hold. A product already in the app's own catalogue, or a complete product you have scanned before, is resolved on the phone with no request at all. A product that came back incomplete, or that Open Food Facts did not recognise, is looked up again the next time you scan it, unless you completed it from the pack and saved it.

What is sent. The barcode number, and a User-Agent line naming the app and the support address in section 1. Nothing else: no food log, no profile, no weight, no device identifier, no advertising identifier, no location and no Friends account.

The product photograph. When a scan finds a product, the app also asks Open Food Facts for that product's photograph, so you can check you have the right item before logging it. That is a second request, to the same organisation, carrying only the address of the picture. It sends nothing about you and nothing you have logged. It happens only after a scan you started, never in the background, and never for a product this phone already holds. Product images are contributed by the public under a CC BY-SA licence.

Who receives it, and where. Open Food Facts, at openfoodfacts.org, a free and open food database run by the non-profit Open Food Facts association in France. This is a disclosure of information to an overseas recipient. Open Food Facts is an independent organisation, not our contractor and not under our control. It decides its own handling, logging and retention under its own privacy policy and terms, and under French and European law rather than Australian law. We do not control, and cannot undertake to you, how it handles a request once it arrives. By scanning a barcode you consent to that disclosure. If you do not want this barcode request sent, do not use the scanner.

What comes back, and its limits. Open Food Facts is contributed by members of the public. A product's name, brand, serving size and nutrition figures may be incomplete, out of date or simply wrong, and a product may be missing entirely. The information returned by a scan is not verified by us and is not a guarantee of what is in the product. The package label is authoritative. Anyone managing an allergy, intolerance, medical condition or anything else where an error would matter must read the current package label every time and must not rely on a scanned result. Any dietary tags shown in the app, such as gluten-free or dairy-free, are catalogue hints from the same sources and carry the same limits. A scan that finds nothing simply returns you to entering the food by hand.

A result is stored on your phone so the same product resolves offline next time. You can edit or delete it like any other food, and "Delete everything" in Settings removes it with the rest.

Automated text filter. Text you write that other people in Friends can see, such as your Friends name, chat messages, the names in workouts you share and continuing-circle names, is checked by an automated word filter: first in the app, before anything is sent, and again by our service before anything is saved. The filter looks for slurs and hate terms (including racial, ethnic, religious, homophobic, transphobic and ableist terms), explicit sexual terms, threats, and words encouraging self-harm, including common disguised spellings. Ordinary swearing is not filtered. If the filter finds a match, the text is not sent or saved, and you are asked to change it. The filter is a fixed list of words and patterns. It runs only in the app and on our service, and no text is sent to anyone else to be checked. It can miss things and can sometimes refuse harmless text, so reports, blocking and the beta operator's review remain in place.

Continuing-circle goals. A continuing circle keeps the same group from week to week. Joining it alone does not join a goal or share activity. The owner can set seven-day step or training-day goals and change their targets without replacing the circle or its members. A new goal can begin from tomorrow to 14 days ahead, in the circle's time zone. Goals of the same kind cannot overlap.

You separately choose whether to carry step sharing or training-day sharing forward in that circle. This ongoing choice joins you to its current and future goals of that kind. It shares eligible daily phone step totals or days with a completed in-app workout with current and future members participating in those goals, subject to the existing block and access controls. Only activity after your agreement is eligible. Changing a target does not expand your permission. Routes, heart rate, food, weight and Cycle records are not included. Chat, Train together and Training now each still require their own agreement.

We store the circle, goal, dates, target and your ongoing sharing choice, including its kind, consent version and agreement time. Goal progress uses the same daily records and retention rules as weekly circles. The beta operator can access these records. Goal records are removed with the underlying week under the existing weekly-circle clean-up rules; ending a goal does not remove the continuing circle or its members.

Turning ongoing sharing off removes your progress from that circle's linked goals of that kind and stops future sharing, without removing you from the circle. Leaving or being removed from the circle ends its ongoing sharing choices. Suspending your Friends access also removes these choices and linked progress. Switching continuing circles off, or pausing or ending the beta, removes all ongoing goal-sharing choices and linked progress; sharing must be chosen again if access returns. A separately paused week stays paused when you choose ongoing sharing. Delete my Friends data also removes your ongoing sharing records under the existing deletion rules.

Training now in weekly circles. While a workout is under way in Lift & Fuel (a strength session, a cardio timer, or a run or walk the app is recording), you can choose to show a weekly circle that you are training now, during that circle's week. You pick one of 12 fixed labels: Training, Strength, Push, Pull, Legs, Upper body, Lower body, Full body, Cardio, Running, Walking or Cycling. You agree each time, for that session in that circle. Our service stores the label, a random reference made for this sharing, the sharing agreement it comes under, when you started sharing, when it was last renewed, when it lapses and the version of the Training now agreement you accepted, with your Friends account and the circle. Members sharing that week, including you, can see your Friends name, avatar, the label, when your status last updated and when it will lapse, unless one of you has blocked the other or your Friends access is suspended. In the last two minutes before the two-hour limit, the lapse time also shows when you started sharing. The beta operator can also see it. Training now never includes your workout's name, exercises, sets, weights, location, route, heart rate, Apple Health data or a device identifier. It does not count as a training day, does not change any score, sends no notifications and does not appear in widgets or exports.

While the app is open, it renews your status about every 45 seconds, and each renewal lasts two minutes. Your status ends two minutes after its last renewal, two hours after you started sharing it, at the end of the week, or when you stop sharing it, whichever comes first. Locking your phone, leaving the app or losing your connection stops the renewals, so your status can end before your workout does. An ended status cannot be renewed; to show one again, you choose again. You can always stop sharing your status, whatever else is switched off, and stopping deletes it.

So that a delayed or repeated request cannot bring back a status you stopped, our service keeps a receipt for each status you start or stop: the circle, your account, the random reference and the time, but not the label. It keeps at most 300 receipts for each circle you are in. Receipts are deleted once they are 24 hours old, at your next status update in that circle or by our daily clean-up, whichever comes first. A status that has lapsed is no longer shown, and is deleted at your next status update in that circle or by our daily clean-up. Stopping sharing with that week or leaving it deletes your status and receipts there. If we switch Training now off, pause or end the beta, or suspend your Friends access, the statuses affected are deleted at once and do not come back. Statuses and receipts are also deleted with the week's circle and with your Friends account.

Chat in your continuing circle. You may separately opt into text-only chat in a continuing circle. Chat belongs to the circle, not to its weekly goal: a target change or ownership transfer does not replace its conversation or give anyone new sharing permission. Joining a continuing circle, sharing goal progress and sharing training plans do not turn chat on. Weekly-circle chat agreements do not carry into a continuing circle.

Current members with access to Friends who have chosen chat can see eligible messages from other consenting current members. Messages from before your membership or your latest chat agreement are not shown. Blocking hides each person's messages from the other. The beta operator can access stored messages and reports. The existing text limits and on-device and server filters apply; they are not a guarantee that every link, contact detail or unsafe message will be caught. No text is sent to an outside AI moderation service.

Messages become invisible after 30 days; stored messages and their retry receipts are removed by scheduled cleanup after that point. Turning chat off, leaving or being removed deletes your messages and retry receipts from that circle. Membership renewal or suspension invalidates the old agreement and removes your messages and retry receipts. Pausing the beta, continuing circles or chat ends the applicable chat agreements; reopening does not automatically opt anyone back in. A service pause retains messages within their retention window for own-only management. While access is paused, a current member may view and delete only their own retained messages and withdraw chat consent; they cannot read peers' messages or send new ones.

Reported messages use the same moderation inbox as weekly-circle reports. A report retains its text copy, sender, reporter, reason and relevant times even if the original message is deleted. It remains until review and becomes eligible for scheduled deletion six months afterwards, unless either person's Friends account is deleted sooner. Retry receipts contain no message text. Chat agreement records follow the continuing membership's retention rules. Optional chat badges and phone alerts are described below.

Chat badges while you use Friends. A circle's unread badge counts eligible messages from other people received during the current circle visit. It is not a complete count of messages missed while the app was closed. Opening Chat marks messages displayed there as seen for that visit. Message text is held in app memory while that circle's chat controller is active, including when switching between its Overview, Chat and Settings sections. It is cleared when the app goes into the background or you leave the circle view. A temporary numeric marker can remain in memory while the same signed-in circle visit is in the background. It is not a saved message archive or a read receipt shared with other members and does not survive closing the app. Sign-out, loss of access and withdrawal clear this temporary state.

Optional phone alerts for Circle chat. Where available, you may separately enable chat alerts for a circle on this phone and allow iOS notifications. Joining a circle or turning chat on does not enable phone alerts. Your choice on one phone does not enable alerts on another. You can turn a circle's alerts off in its settings without leaving the circle or ending chat. If no circles remain enabled, this phone's chat notification registration is removed. iOS Settings can also prevent alerts from being shown.

The service stores your Friends account, an account-specific random installation identifier, Apple's device push token, its delivery environment, update and revision information, and this phone's separate circle alert preferences. It records the latest eligible message sequence marked read on this installation to suppress already-read alerts and calculate its notification badge. This is a numeric position, not a copy of the conversation, and is not shown to other members. This phone's system badge counts only eligible unread messages in circles for which this phone has enabled alerts; it may differ from the current-visit circle badge or another phone's count.

Our Sydney Supabase service sends alerts directly through Apple Push Notification service, not through Expo's push relay. Apple receives the push token, our app identifier, a generic “New message in Friends” alert, the numeric badge and delivery information. The payload does not contain message text, a person's name, a circle name or identifier, a workout name, routes, heart rate, food, weight or Cycle information. Apple processes delivery under its own privacy practices and may process this information outside Australia. A generic alert on the Lock Screen can still reveal that you use Lift & Fuel and have Friends activity.

Delivery jobs store internal references and retry information, not a message body or health records. Jobs stop being eligible after 24 hours and are removed by daily cleanup. Device registrations and security records used to reject delayed changes stop being eligible after 90 days without an update and are then removed by daily cleanup. Unregistering removes the token, this installation's alert preferences and queued jobs, but retains a token-free revision record within that 90-day limit to reject stale requests. Deleting the Friends account removes these records. Disabling alerts does not delete chat messages; chat deletion and retention remain as described above.

Before sending, the service checks current membership, chat agreement, blocking, service availability and this installation's alert preference. Muting, withdrawing, leaving, removal and deletion prevent queued alerts once the server confirms the change. Sign-out immediately stops local notification work and attempts to unregister the old account's device; if the phone is offline, server revocation cannot be confirmed immediately. Already-delivered or Apple-accepted generic alerts cannot be recalled. iOS, connectivity and retry behaviour can delay, combine, duplicate or suppress alerts; alerts are not guaranteed real-time or emergency messages. Tapping an alert opens Friends after the app's usual access checks; the alert itself grants no access.

Training now in your continuing circle. During a session in Lift & Fuel, you may separately choose to share one fixed training label with current members of a continuing circle. This choice is for that session and circle only, regardless of whether a weekly goal is active. It shows your Friends name, avatar, chosen label and update and expiry times. It does not send your workout name, exercises, sets, weights, location, route, heart rate, food, weight records or Cycle records.

Status renewals are sent while the app is active. A status becomes invisible after two minutes without a renewal and can never continue beyond two hours from its original start. Locking the phone, losing connection or ending the session can end it sooner. Leaving, removal, suspension or a service pause clears the affected status. Reopening the app or changing a goal does not grant a new session-sharing agreement.

The server keeps the circle and account, a random session reference, chosen label, start, update and expiry times, and consent version. Expired status rows are removed on the next applicable update or scheduled cleanup. Text-free session receipts prevent duplicate or delayed starts within their retention window and are removed on the next applicable update or scheduled cleanup after 24 hours. Account deletion removes these records. The beta operator can access them. Statuses do not go to widgets, exports or push notifications.

Limits on how often you can act. To limit misuse, our service counts some actions for each Friends account, for example chat messages sent in a minute and in a day, changes to continuing circles in an hour, and Training now starts and renewals in an hour (at most 1,800). A counter holds only your account, what it counts, when its counting period began and the count. Each counter is deleted by our daily clean-up once 24 hours have passed since its latest counting period began, and with your Friends account. Turning chat off, deleting your own messages, leaving a continuing circle, turning Train together off and stopping Training now are never counted or refused for this reason.

Reports about a person. When you report someone, the report records you, them, the reason you chose from a fixed list and when; there is no free text. If you are in a continuing circle with them, or were in the last 30 days, the report also keeps a copy of up to 12 such circles as they are at that moment: each circle's name and icon, and whether they owned it, so that a later change cannot alter what is reviewed. Reports and their copies are kept until the beta operator has reviewed them, and are deleted six months after that review, or sooner if your Friends account or theirs is deleted.

9. Retention and deletion

On-device records remain until you delete entries or uninstall the app. Cycle records can be deleted on their own with “Delete cycle history” in the Cycle card. Export records you want to keep first. “Delete everything” in Settings erases the app's databases, including the separate one holding information read from Apple Health, and the automatic on-phone backup files it keeps in the app's Documents folder; a copy inside your device's own iCloud or computer backup remains under Apple's control until that backup is replaced. Information read from Apple Health is not in those backups. Deleting a run removes its local route and native record, and a durable deletion receipt removes its Cardio projection when the app resumes. A local deletion receipt prevents the same Health source workout being automatically imported again while that receipt remains. Delete everything also turns automatic run sync off, clears its consent, native run files and deletion receipts, and invalidates the paired Watch command history. If you later re-enable imports, eligible workouts still in Health can be read again. It does not delete saved Apple Health workouts or copies you shared. Friends account/server deletion is a separate action in Friends, not an automatic result of uninstalling or clearing local app data. Support correspondence is retained only as reasonably necessary for support, security, legal or record-keeping purposes: for up to 24 months after your most recent message, unless a longer period is required by law.

Personal logs remain on-device except for copies you deliberately share and the limited, separately consented Friends information described above. We use on-device storage, account-scoped Friends permissions and release security checks. No device or storage system is completely secure, so protect your phone with a passcode and keep its operating system current.
