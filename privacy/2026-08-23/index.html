Privacy Policy

Effective 23 August 2026

In short: your log lives on your phone. There is no Lift & Fuel account, advertising, analytics or behavioural tracking. If you enable Server Sync, the app sends a backup to the server you choose and downloads approved reference updates.

1. Who is responsible

This policy explains how Emmanuel Omar, trading as Emmanuel Omar, ABN not applicable, of 10 Adam Place, Macquarie Fields NSW 2564 (we, us or our), handles information in connection with Lift & Fuel. Privacy contact: lift.fuel@outlook.com.

2. Information the app handles

Depending on what you choose to enter, the app may handle:

  • profile details such as name, age, sex, height, weight, body-fat estimate and goals;
  • food, calorie, macro, water, supplement and reminder records;
  • exercise routines, training sets, cardio, recovery activities and readiness estimates;
  • weigh-ins, preferences and the version and time of your legal acceptance;
  • an opaque device identifier and technical sync status if Server Sync is configured; and
  • support messages and contact details you voluntarily send outside the app.

Some entries may amount to health information or other sensitive information under applicable law. Do not enter information about another person unless you are authorised to do so.

3. How information is collected

App information is collected directly from what you type, select or log. If you grant permission, Lift & Fuel reads two things from Apple Health: your body weight, to help work out your daily energy use, and how long you slept last night, which is shown on the Recover screen and used in its readiness estimate. It writes the weigh-ins and the strength workouts you record back to Apple Health so your other apps can see them. Health information read from Apple Health stays on your device, is never included in a server backup and is never sent anywhere by the app. Lift & Fuel does not access your contacts or precise location. It does not use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs or cross-app tracking. Notification permission is requested only when you choose to schedule reminders.

4. How information is used

The app uses information to display your log, calculate general fitness and nutrition estimates, show trends and readiness, schedule local reminders, create your requested export, maintain app integrity and, if enabled, perform Server Sync. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

5. Storage on your phone

Your profile and logs are stored in the app's database on your device. The sync token is stored separately in the operating system's secure credential storage and is marked for that device only. It is not placed in the app database, user export or server backup. Protect your phone with a passcode and keep its operating system current.

6. Optional Server Sync

Server Sync is off by default. If you turn it on, the app connects to the address you provide, authenticates using the token you provide, downloads approved food and exercise reference data, and sends append-only snapshots of your personal app data. The app requires HTTPS for a public server; it also permits a private Tailscale or local-network connection.

The standard Lift & Fuel server retains the newest 60 snapshots for each device and rejects a sudden reduction of more than half unless the server owner expressly approves it. It cannot silently write a backup over the phone's log. Legal-acceptance records are included in your own export but are not sent in the optional server snapshot.

If you select a server operated by you or another person, that server's operator controls its location, access, logs, retention and any further disclosure. We do not control a third-party or self-hosted server merely because the app can connect to it. If we offer an operator-controlled server, its location and any overseas disclosures must be added here before use: None. We do not offer an operator-controlled server, so we make no overseas disclosure of your personal information; Server Sync connects only to a server you choose and control.

7. Other disclosures

With Server Sync off, the app makes network requests in only two situations, both started by you. First, if you scan a barcode the app does not already know, it sends that barcode number to the Open Food Facts database (openfoodfacts.org, operated in France by the non-profit Open Food Facts association) to look up the product, identifying itself with the app name and the support email address in section 12. Nothing else is sent: no log data, no profile, no identifier, no account. The result is stored on your phone so the same product resolves offline next time, and a scan that finds nothing simply returns you to manual entry. Second, opening the hosted Terms or Privacy Policy uses your device's browser and necessarily gives the web host ordinary connection information, such as IP address and browser details. Local notification text may appear on a locked screen according to your iOS notification settings. We may disclose information where required by law, to address a serious threat where law permits, or with your consent.

8. Retention and deletion

On-device records remain until you delete entries or uninstall the app. Export records you want to keep first. “Delete everything” in Settings erases the app's database and the automatic on-phone backup files it keeps in the app's Documents folder; a copy inside your device's own iCloud or computer backup remains under Apple's control until that backup is replaced. Turning sync off prevents further app network calls. “Forget this server and token” removes the stored server address and credential from the phone, but does not delete snapshots already held by that server. Contact the relevant server owner to delete those copies. Support correspondence is retained only as reasonably necessary for support, security, legal or record-keeping purposes: for up to 24 months after your most recent message, unless a longer period is required by law.

9. Access, correction and portability

You can view and correct most app information directly and use “Export everything” to obtain the data held by the app. For information we separately hold through support or an operator-controlled server, request access or correction at lift.fuel@outlook.com. We may need to verify the request and may refuse it where permitted or required by law, explaining the reason where appropriate.

10. Security

We use measures including on-device app storage, secure credential storage, scoped server tokens, transport rules, bounded server backups and release security checks. No device, transmission or storage system is completely secure. Tell us promptly if you believe information associated with an operator-controlled service has been compromised.

11. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. A person under 18 should use it with a parent or guardian where appropriate and should not rely on its estimates as individual health or dietary advice. A lawyer must confirm the operator's consent and notice process before the app is marketed to children.

12. Questions and complaints

Contact lift.fuel@outlook.com with a privacy question or complaint. We aim to acknowledge it within 5 business days and provide a substantive response within a reasonable period. If the Privacy Act applies and you are not satisfied, you may be entitled to contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

13. Changes

We will keep this policy accurate and publish each material version at its own URL. If handling practices or the policy change materially, the app will require review of the new version before affected logging or sync continues. Declining an update does not itself erase existing local data.
